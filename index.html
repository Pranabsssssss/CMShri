Institutional Continuity

More Than a School. A Legacy of Learning.

For over two decades, the Sector 10, Dwarka campus has stood as a benchmark for public education in India. While its official title has evolved across institutional milestones, its core mission—nurturing gifted students with uncompromising academic standards—remains unwavering.

Originally founded as Rajkiya Pratibha Vikas Vidyalaya (RPVV), the institution consistently led Delhi Government schools in CBSE board examinations and earned multiple first-place national rankings. Later designated as Dr. B. R. Ambedkar School of Specialized Excellence (SOSE) with a focus on STEM and international experiential learning, the campus is today officially designated as Dr. B. R. Ambedkar CM SHRI School under the flagship school transformation framework.

The Directorate of Education, Government of NCT of Delhi, records both historic and current iterations under the same unified campus building ID (18211038) and geographic coordinates in Dwarka Sector 10.
